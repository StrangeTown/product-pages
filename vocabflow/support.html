背背词

背背词用真实的对话和场景来记英文：把原文贴进来，标出要记住的词，回顾时遮住再刮开，想起来了才算记住。

遇到问题或有建议，欢迎发邮件到 yinxingdyx@163.com。方便的话请附上 iPhone 型号、iOS 版本和问题截图。

常见问题

怎么录入一个情景？

在首页点录入，先把原文贴进来，配图可选；点「下一步」后，长按原文选词，拖两头的标记调整范围，标出要记住的部分，每处可以写一句描述或释义，最后保存。

能不能一次性贴进来已经标好的内容？

可以。在原文里把要记住的词用 [[ ]] 括起来，| 后面写释义（可不写），例如：

Could you grab me a [[flat white|澳白，一种奶咖]] on your way in?

点「下一步」会自动标好，在第 2 步确认后保存。录入页的「带标注贴入」里有一段提示词，可以直接复制给 AI，让它按这个格式写。

怎么删除一个情景？

点首页频率分布右边的「共 N 个」进入全部情景，在里面删除；或者回顾时编辑这个情景，在编辑页右上角删除。删除后原文、配图、标注和描述都会删掉，不能恢复。

换手机了，数据怎么办？

背背词没有账号和云同步，数据只存在设备上。用 iCloud 云备份或电脑备份恢复到新手机时，数据会一起迁移过去。删除应用会清除全部数据，请先确认已有备份。

朗读没有声音？

请检查手机是否开了静音、媒体音量是否调低。朗读使用的是系统语音，可以在 iPhone「设置 → 辅助功能 → 朗读内容 → 声音」里下载更清晰的英文语音。

FAQ

How do I add a situation?

Tap “录入” (Add) on the home screen and paste in the passage; an image is optional. Tap “下一步” (Next), then long-press the text to select words and drag the handles at either end to adjust the range. Mark each part you want to remember, optionally add a description or definition, and save.

Can I paste in text that is already marked?

Yes. Wrap each word to remember in [[ ]], and optionally add a definition after |, for example:

Could you grab me a [[flat white|a coffee with steamed milk]] on your way in?

Tap “下一步” (Next) and the marks are filled in automatically; review them in step 2 and save. “带标注贴入” (Paste with marks) on the add screen has a prompt you can copy and give to an AI so it writes in this format.

How do I delete a situation?

On the home screen, tap “共 N 个” (N total) next to the frequency chart to open the full list and delete it there. You can also edit the situation during review and delete it from the top-right corner of the edit screen. Deleting removes its passage, image, marks, and descriptions permanently.

What happens to my data when I switch phones?

背背词 has no accounts or cloud sync; your data lives only on your device. When you restore a new phone from an iCloud or computer backup, your data comes along. Deleting the app erases all data, so make sure you have a backup first.

Read aloud makes no sound

Check that your phone isn't in silent mode and that media volume isn't turned down. Read aloud uses the system voice; you can download a clearer English voice in Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices.
